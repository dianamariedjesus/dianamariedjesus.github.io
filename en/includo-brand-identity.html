THE CHALLENGE

IncluDO was born to combat the depopulation of historic Italian villages and preserve ancient craft traditions at risk of extinction. Without an established visual identity, the non-profit had no way to raise awareness and convey its mission. My role was to create a cohesive, empathetic, and scalable visual system from scratch, capable of communicating both the value of artisanal craftwork and social inclusion.

Context

Founded by two non-profit professionals to combat village depopulation and preserve historic trades, IncluDO offers free courses to pass local craftsmanship on to young people, migrants, and underprivileged individuals.

What Was Missing

A recognizable brand identity capable of engaging the local community for courses and workshops, as well as attracting partners, sponsors, and funding institutions.

Requirements & Constraints

Designing a scalable logo (legible even at small sizes and in monochrome), a comprehensive color palette with exact values, typographic guidelines, an icon set, and social media promotional graphics (Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube).

VISUAL DESIGN PROCESS

I developed the visual identity around four core values: support, diversity, inclusion, and artisanal training. Every visual asset—from the naming to the color palette—was crafted to blend human warmth with visual rigor. I aimed for a contemporary design language that celebrates tradition without feeling outdated or trapped in the past.

Naming & Meaning

"IncluDO" combines the concept of inclusion with the English verb "Do" (the hands-on action of craftsmanship), while echoing the Italian first-person "io includo" (I include) to emphasize a vision where no one is left behind.

Brandmark & Symbol

I customized the final letter "O" by transforming it into two interlocking hands within a circle. This represents the coming together of diverse people and cultures, alongside the tactile craft of raw materials, also functioning as a standalone icon for social media avatars.

Brand Architecture: A combination of logotype, symbol, and tagline, featuring an independent icon optimized for avatars and constrained spaces.
Scalability System & Color Variants: Primary, inverted, and monochrome (B&W) variations designed to ensure high contrast and legibility across all mediums.

Typography & Hierarchy

I selected the sans-serif font family Raleway to ensure clean legibility across all screens, utilizing distinct weights (Black for primary headlines and lighter weights for subtitles and body copy) to establish a clear, structured hierarchy.

Typographic System with Raleway: A scalable visual hierarchy utilizing distinct font weights (Black, Bold, SemiBold, Medium) to guarantee optimal readability across digital interfaces and print collateral.

Color Palette

  • Brown (#552220): Primary brand color evoking earth, wood, and artisanal terracotta workshops.
  • Blue (#3B729B) & Green (#708B63): Symbolizing the interlocking hands, representing oceans and continents as a metaphor for cultural exchange and nature.
  • Orange (#E28C5A): Brings warmth, vitality, and reflects the tones and cultural traditions of the region.
  • Soft Yellow (#F9EFC5): Adds light and dynamism, conveying openness and trust.

Brand Color Palette: A system of primary, secondary, and accent colors with precise specifications (HEX, RGB, CMYK) designed to unite earthy warmth, nature, and energy.

Custom Icon Pack

I designed 8 custom vector line icons to represent the craft workshops: Goldsmithing, Ceramics, Sewing, Weaving, Food Handling & Safety, Agri-food, Shoemaking, Metal & Woodworking.

Set of 8 Custom Vector Icons for Artisanal Tracks: A consistent linear style and shared geometric grid ensuring instant visual recognition and intuitive course navigation.

Decisions & Trade-offs

I made the deliberate choice to avoid vintage or overly rustic illustrations to keep the village brand from feeling stuck in the past; instead, I prioritized a clean, modern visual aesthetic that resonates effectively with public institutions and corporate sponsors.

Instagram — Promotional Post Set (Mobile-First): High-impact Instagram feed templates (1080x1350 px) structured with clear visual hierarchy for workshop and local event launches.

IMPACT & TAKEAWAYS

This project demonstrates how visual design can champion ethical and social causes: a clear, accessible brand identity doesn't just polish an interface—it lowers cognitive load and makes services instantly understandable at first glance.
