THE CHALLENGE

This project was born as a natural continuation of the visual identity work done for IncluDO. The brief called for a long-form blog article; I used this opportunity to explore how writing can build a bridge of trust, transforming a complex topic like the decline of local craftsmanship into an engaging, accessible awareness journey for readers.

Context

IncluDO is a non-profit founded to combat the depopulation of historic villages and prevent the closure of traditional craft workshops.

The initiative offers free courses to pass artisanal trades on to youth, migrants, and disadvantaged groups.

Key Objective

It wasn't enough to describe the organization's activities in a dry, institutional tone.

The goal was to craft a blog article that captivates readers and guides them toward meaningful action—such as enrolling in courses, purchasing from the non-profit shop, or volunteering.

Requirements & Scope

The brief required an in-depth article (1,500+ words) complete with target audience definition, distinct tone of voice, authoritative source citations, and practical application of persuasion principles and Call to Action (CTA) strategies.

STRATEGY & DESIGN CHOICES

To transform a dense subject into an engaging read, I structured the article combining UX writing techniques and behavioral psychology. I refined the visual hierarchy to avoid overwhelming the reader and seamlessly integrated Cialdini's principles of persuasion throughout the narrative.

The goal was to inform transparently and focus on actionable solutions without ever resorting to pity or overly dramatic tones regarding the crisis in historic villages.

Target Audience

The article speaks to a broad audience (aged 18+) that values handmade craftsmanship, volunteering, and social inclusion, and is eager to support tangible community initiatives.

Headline & Subheadline

For the opening, I crafted a headline designed to spark curiosity around the hidden value of artisanal work ("Rediscovering the Value of Artisanal Trades: The Inspiring Story of IncluDO"), paired with an explanatory subheadline that highlights the convergence of tradition and inclusion to set clear reader expectations.

Article cover featuring the Headline and Subheadline, crafted to communicate the core value proposition within the first 5 seconds and minimize bounce rates.

UX Writing & Layout

Structuring content clearly is essential to help readers navigate without cognitive fatigue. To achieve this, I applied a clean, scannable layout:

Upfront Table of Contents

Allows readers to quickly orient themselves among core themes and jump directly to sections of interest.

Short Paragraphs & White Space

Text blocks kept to a maximum of 3–4 lines separated by generous white space to optimize scannability and eliminate "wall-of-text" fatigue.

Engaging Visual & Copy Elements

Rhetorical questions addressing the reader directly to encourage reflection, contextual imagery supporting key narratives, and strategic bolding to guide the eye toward core takeaways.

Persuasion Principles (Cialdini)

Effective copy doesn't just inform—it leads readers through a logical and emotional journey that motivates them to take concrete action. To build trust and engagement, I incorporated several proven persuasion principles:

Authority: Cited official national labor statistics on the decline of artisanal workshops to highlight the real-world scale of the challenge.
Scarcity & Urgency: Added application deadlines for training courses with limited available spots, encouraging prompt decision-making.
Reciprocity: Featured an exclusive 30% discount on handmade products for users who subscribe to the newsletter.
Social Proof: Displayed real social engagement metrics and shares to reassure new readers of the content's quality and credibility.

Value Proposition

Every section was structured to guide the reader toward concrete engagement: supporting the non-profit by enrolling in workshops, purchasing craft products from the online shop, or applying as a volunteer.

Decisions & Trade-offs

Throughout the writing process, I chose to prioritize clarity and accessible language over academic or overly technical jargon. The main trade-off was balancing an in-depth exploration of the rural crisis with short, digestible paragraphs to maintain high reader retention.

RESULTS & DELIVERABLES

The final deliverable consists of a 1,554-word blog article accompanied by a comprehensive Content Strategy document that maps out the practical application of persuasion triggers and Call to Action placements.

The Blog Article
A complete 1,554-word piece (estimated read time: 8 minutes) organized with headline, subheadline, table of contents, dedicated sections, and engaging visual breaks.
Content Strategy Document
In-depth analysis covering audience segmentation, tone of voice guidelines, and a detailed map of the 4 persuasion principles applied in the text.
Calls to Action & Touchpoints
6 distinct CTA formats placed organically across the article to drive course enrollment, ethical e-commerce sales, and volunteer sign-ups.

WHAT I LEARNED

This project reinforced that copywriting and UX writing are essential pillars of product design: translating complex challenges into clear, empathetic narratives helps users build trust and interact effortlessly with the brand.
