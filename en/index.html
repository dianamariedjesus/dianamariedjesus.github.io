Design without empathy is just decoration.

I'm Diana de Jesus. My passion for design didn't come from wanting to make "pretty websites", but from a deep curiosity to understand how things work and how people interact with technology. At start2impact University, I turned this drive into solid skills in UX/UI Design, user research, and information architecture.

I know firsthand what it feels like to struggle with slow, confusing, or poorly designed platforms. Rather than settling for frustrating tech, I chose to design better experiences myself—putting usability and accessibility at the heart of every decision.

I don't just draw screens on Figma: I shape the entire visual and experiential journey of your product. I dig into real user needs to build simple, inclusive, and human-centered interfaces, eliminating navigation friction.
